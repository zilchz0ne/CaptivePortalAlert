Public Wi-Fi networks at coffee shops, airports, hotels, and venues often force you to sign in through a webpage called a captive portal. While many are legitimate, malicious actors can easily create rogue access points ("evil twin" attacks) with fake login pages designed to capture your credentials or intercept your traffic.

Public Wi-Fi Phishing Alert automatically detects when your browser is redirected to a captive portal, giving you an immediate warning before you log in or share sensitive information.


KEY FEATURES

• Instant Captive Portal Detection: Scans connections on public networks and alerts you the moment a portal prompt or network redirect occurs.
• Evil Twin Defense: Helps you spot suspicious login portals before entering personal information or social sign-ins.
• Privacy-First Design: Operates locally on your device without sending your browsing data or network traffic to external servers.
• Lightweight & Efficient: Runs seamlessly in the background using minimal browser resources.


HOW IT WORKS

1. Connect to any public Wi-Fi access point.
2. If the network redirects you to a captive portal page, Public Wi-Fi Phishing Alert triggers a clear warning notification.
3. Review the network page safely before entering passwords, emails, or credentials.


WHO IS THIS FOR?

• Frequent travelers using airport, train, or hotel Wi-Fi.
• Remote workers and students connecting in cafes or co-working spaces.
• Security-conscious users who want an added layer of network awareness.


PRIVACY & TRANSPARENCY

Your privacy matters. Public Wi-Fi Phishing Alert:
• Does NOT track, log, or sell your browsing history.
• Does NOT require unnecessary site access permissions.
• Performs all detection routines locally inside your browser.
