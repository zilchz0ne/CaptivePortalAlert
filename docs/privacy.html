Privacy Policy for CaptivePortalAlert

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

CaptivePortalAlert is a security-focused open-source Chrome extension designed to detect network-level captive portal redirects and alert users to potential risks on unencrypted public Wi-Fi networks.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We collect zero user data. CaptivePortalAlert does not record, log, store, or transmit any user activity, browsing history, IP addresses, credentials, or personal information.

3. Permissions Justification

To perform network detection, the extension uses specific Chrome permissions solely inside your local browser:

  • webNavigation: Detects initial network probes to connectivity endpoints (e.g., generate_204) and flags redirected tab IDs locally in memory.
  • scripting & <all_urls>: Injects a visual alert banner (CSS/JS) into captive portal login pages to notify you of an untrusted environment.

4. Network Requests

The extension sends a stateless, lightweight background probe to http://connectivitycheck.gstatic.com/generate_204 solely to verify network status. No personal identifiers or cookies are included in this check.
